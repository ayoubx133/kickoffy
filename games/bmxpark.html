About the game

Your rider pedals on their own, you do the tricks. Hold in the air to flip, let go to line up the wheels and stomp the landing. Tap on the ground to hop over logs and onto rails, then grind to the end. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds: the skate park, dirt trails, city rails, a sunset mega park with bounce pads and a night session where you learn frontflips. Earn coins and unlock new riders and bikes.

  • One finger BMX tricks with real flip physics
  • Kickers, gaps, logs, rails and bounce pads
  • Course map, trick combos and medal ceremonies
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
