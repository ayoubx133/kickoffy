About the game

Tap when the ball hits the green zone to pull a strong stroke and race down your lane. Tap breathe before your air runs out, flip turn just before the wall, wait for the beep to dive and duck under waves. Beat the CPU swimmers to win gold. 100 levels in 5 worlds, from the big pool to a wave night.

  • Rhythm swimming: tap on the beat for perfect strokes
  • Breathing, flip turns, dive starts and waves
  • Race CPU swimmers for gold, silver and bronze
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
