About the game

Drag back to set the angle and power of your swing, then let go to drive the ball down the course. Watch the wind arrow, stay out of bunkers and ponds, loft your ball over tall trees and deep gaps, and roll your putt into the cup. Finish each hole in par to beat your rival and win the trophy. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from green hills to night sky islands.

  • 100 golf holes in 5 worlds with their own twist
  • Wind, ponds, ice, trees, gaps and bounce pads
  • Unlock golfers, balls and clubs in the locker room
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
