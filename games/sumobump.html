About the game

Put on a giant bouncy sumo suit and push your rival out of the ring. Tap when the rings meet for a perfect bump, hold to brace against red charges, swipe to dodge belly slams and grab power buns to go giant. Fight through 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a cozy dojo to a cherry garden, a lantern festival and a moonlit tag team tourney. It is silly, bouncy fun for the whole family with no injuries, just big cartoon bumps.

  • Rhythm taps for perfect belly bumps
  • Brace, dodge and go giant with power buns
  • Tag team bouts against wobbly cartoon rivals
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
