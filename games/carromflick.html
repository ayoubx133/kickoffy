About the game

Carrom is the classic board game where you flick a striker to knock coins into the corner pockets. Slide the striker along the bar, pull back on the board and let go to flick. Pocket all your white coins before the rival pockets the black ones, and grab the red queen, then cover it with your next coin. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds: the Sunny Veranda, the Beach Picnic, the Palace Hall, the Sunset Beach and Lantern Night, with brass posts, slippy powder, slow mats and a moving pocket lid.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new board twists
  • Smooth flick physics with a clear aim line
  • Unlock 6 striker designs with your coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
