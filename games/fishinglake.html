About the game

Hold to swing the power and let go to cast where the fish swim. Tap right when a fish bites, then hold and release to reel it in while keeping the line tension in the green. Fill your catch list across 100 levels in 5 worlds: a calm lake, a sea pier full of junk to dodge, an ice hole where you drop the line, a sunset bay with fish that make wild runs and a dark night pier with glowing fish. Collect cute species and unlock fun angler skins.

  • Easy cast, hook and reel controls
  • Over 15 cute fish species to catch
  • Ice fishing, junk, fish runs and glowing night fish
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
