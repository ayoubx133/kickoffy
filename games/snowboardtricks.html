About the game

Drag left and right to carve down snowy slopes. Ride over kickers and swipe in the air to spin, flip and grab, then land clean before you touch the snow. Grind rails, steer through slalom gates, cross ice bridges and dodge rolling snowballs to reach the trick goal. 100 levels in 5 worlds, from pine slopes to a night run.

  • Swipe tricks: spins, backflips and grabs with combos
  • Rails, slalom gates, ice bridges and snowballs
  • Course map shows every run before you ride
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
