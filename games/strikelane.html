About the game

Swipe up to roll the ball down the lane and bend your swipe to add a hook. Knock down every pin in the frame to win: full racks, tricky splits, pin rings and walls. Bowl around rubber bumpers, time your roll past moving blockers, ride sideways slopes and hunt the golden pins. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from classic lanes to a glowing night alley.

  • 100 bowling lanes in 5 worlds with new twists
  • Real pin physics: strikes, spares and flying pins
  • Unlock bowlers and shiny bowling balls in the locker room
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
