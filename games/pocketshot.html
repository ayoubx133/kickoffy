About the game

Each level is a little pool puzzle. Pull back anywhere on the table and let go to shoot the cue ball, then watch it bank off the rails and knock the colored balls into the pockets. Clear the table within the shot limit, and use fewer shots for 3 stars. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds: the Game Room, the Snowy Cabin, the Desert Canyon, the Sunset Canyon and the Midnight Room, with log walls, portals, bouncy bumpers and switch gates.

  • 100 puzzle levels in 5 worlds
  • Portals, bumpers, log walls and switch gates
  • Unlock 6 fun cue ball designs
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
