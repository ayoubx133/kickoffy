About the game

Drag back from the ball, aim with the dotted guide and let go to putt. Bank shots off the walls, roll past water and sand, time your putt through spinning windmills and ride slopes, bumpers and magic tubes into the cup. Sink it in par or better to earn three medals and coins. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a sunny tropical island to a moonlit bay.

  • 100 handmade mini golf holes in 5 island worlds
  • Windmills, water, slopes, bumpers and magic tubes
  • Unlock new golf balls and golfers in the locker room
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
