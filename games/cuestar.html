About the game

Play friendly 8 ball pool against clever rivals. Drag on the table to aim with the guide line, pull the power bar and let go to shoot. Pot all your solid balls, then sink the black 8 to win the match. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds: the Club Hall, the Cruise Deck, the Neon Arcade, the Golden Hall and the Night Finals, each with a new twist like spin control, glowing pockets, bumper posts and the star pocket.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with a new twist in each
  • Real ball physics with aim guide, power and spin
  • Unlock 6 cool cues with the coins you win
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
