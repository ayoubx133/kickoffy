About the game

Fast neon air hockey for one finger. Drag your mallet around your half of the rink, hit the puck with speed and angle, and slide it into the rival's goal while you guard your own. Win the duel by reaching the goal target first. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds: the Neon Arcade, the Frozen Lake, the Space Station, the Sunset Rink and Night Lights, with ice blocks, power ups like the big mallet and the shield, sliding gates and multi puck madness.

  • 100 duels in 5 glowing worlds
  • Power ups, ice blocks, sliding gates and multi puck
  • Unlock 6 neon mallets with your coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
