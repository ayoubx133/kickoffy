About the game

Swipe up from the ball to kick a field goal: the angle of your swipe aims the kick, the length sets the power and a curved swipe bends the ball in the air. Read the wind flags, curve around training nets, thread swinging rings and time your kicks on moving posts. Make enough kicks in each match to lift the trophy. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from the home opener to the championship night.

  • Swipe to aim, power and curve every kick
  • Wind, snow, nets, rings and moving posts
  • Slow motion on close kicks and big crowd cheers
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
