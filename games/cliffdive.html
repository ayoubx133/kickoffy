About the game

Tap to leap off the edge, hold to tuck and spin, then let go to open up and slice into the water head first or feet first. Judges score every dive, so count your flips and line up a clean entry for three medals. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds: tropical cliffs, a high tower with hoops to fly through, a waterfall lagoon full of seagulls, a sunset springboard and a windy night stadium with a target ring. Earn coins and unlock new divers.

  • One finger flip diving with real spin physics
  • Judges, slow motion splashes and medal ceremonies
  • Hoops, seagulls, springboards and wind to master
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
