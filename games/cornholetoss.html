About the game

Flick up to toss your bean bag in a soft arc. Land it on the board for one point or slide it into the hole for three, and push your own bags in with the next toss. Lean into the beach wind, time your toss for sliding boards, aim for the far board that scores double and knock rival bags out of the way. Beat each rival's score to win the trophy. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a backyard bash to a glowing night party.

  • 100 cornhole rounds in 5 party worlds
  • Real bag slide physics: hole, board and knock ins
  • Unlock tossers and bean bag styles in the locker room
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
