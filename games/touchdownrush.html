About the game

Lead your team on a drive to the end zone. Drag to run, flick to juke past tacklers, drag and let go to pass to an open receiver, follow your blockers and tap to stiff arm. Score before you run out of downs and beat the clock in the title game. Tackles are cartoon bumps with spinning stars. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • Run, juke, pass and stiff arm
  • Smart defenders, blockers and a game clock
  • Retro top down drives with big celebrations
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
