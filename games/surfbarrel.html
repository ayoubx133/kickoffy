About the game

Drag up and down to carve across the wave face. Ride low to go fast and stay ahead of the curl, carve high to slow down and grab the stars. Tuck into the barrel near the curl, steer around rocks and buoys, dive low then carve up hard to launch aerials off the lip. Surf 100 levels in 5 worlds, from big blue waves to sunset barrels, a tropical reef, a stormy swell and moonlight surf. Earn coins and unlock new boards and surfers.

  • Smooth one finger carving with real wave speed
  • Barrels, reef, aerials and rolling foam balls
  • Wave map, slow curl chase and medal ceremonies
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
