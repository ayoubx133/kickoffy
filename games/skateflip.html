About the game

Your skater rolls on their own. Tap to ollie over cones, benches and gaps, then swipe left for a kickflip or right for a heelflip while you are in the air. Ollie onto ledges and rails to grind, launch off kickers for double flips, clear whole stair sets and learn the 360 spin. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from the skate park to a night plaza, earn coins and unlock new decks and skaters.

  • Swipe gestures for kickflips, heelflips and 360 spins
  • Ledges, rails, kickers, gaps and stair sets
  • Course map, trick callouts and medal ceremonies
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
