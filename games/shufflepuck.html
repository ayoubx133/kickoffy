About the game

Pull back and let go to slide your puck down a long shuffleboard table. Land in the 1, 2 and 3 zones, hang over the edge for 4 points and bump the rival's pucks off the end. Beat the CPU across 100 levels in 5 worlds, from an oak club table to a rocking cruise ship, a neon arcade with bumpers, ice and sand strips and a moonlit deck with a moving gate. Clear aiming guides, slow motion on great shots and fun puck skins make every match a treat.

  • Smooth puck physics with bank shots and knock offs
  • Play the CPU or solve trick shot challenges
  • Ship tilt, bumpers, ice strips and moving gates
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
