About the game

Sprint for the try line and drag left and right to dodge tacklers. Tap to pass to your support runner, swipe up to chip kick over a defensive wall, flick to sidestep and tap to dive for the try. Every tackle is a harmless cartoon bump. Score under the posts for three stars. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from the club ground to the grand final.

  • Dodge, pass, chip kick and sidestep
  • Rain, mud, sunset and floodlit matches
  • Dive under the posts for big celebrations
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
