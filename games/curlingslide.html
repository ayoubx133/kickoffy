About the game

Pull back to aim and set the power, then let go to slide your stone down the ice. Rub the screen to sweep and make it go farther. Pick a curl to bend around guard stones, knock red stones out of the house and beat the red team with the last stone. 100 levels in 5 worlds, from an indoor rink to a frozen lake and a night arena.

  • Aim guide plus a house view of every stone
  • Curl, sweep, take outs and matches vs a CPU team
  • Rough and fast ice patches in the night world
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
