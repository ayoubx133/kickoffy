About the game

You are the goalkeeper. Drag the big gloves to where the ball is flying and stop it before it hits the net. Shots come from every angle, then they curve, come two at a time, arrive as crosses and headers, and finally as fiery power shots. Catch the ball for bonus coins and keep a clean sheet in 100 levels across 5 worlds.

  • Curves, crosses, headers and power shots
  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new twists
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
