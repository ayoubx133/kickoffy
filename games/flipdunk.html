About the game

Tap to jump from one trampoline to the next and hold to flip in the air. Let go in time to land on your feet, then fly up to the hoop for a big dunk. Every stage asks for a number of flips, so plan your tricks. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with trampoline towers, sliding trampolines, glow rings and exact flip targets.

  • Tap to jump and hold to flip trick controls
  • 100 levels in 5 worlds from the gym to the circus
  • Slow motion dunks and flip combos
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
