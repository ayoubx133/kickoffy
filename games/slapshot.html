About the game

Drag left and right to skate around defenders, then flick up to shoot. A fast flick sends the puck to the top corner. Find the gap the goalie leaves, hit glowing corner targets, pass to your teammate for one timer goals and beat a giant swaying goalie. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from your home rink to the night finals.

  • Drag to skate and flick to shoot
  • Defenders, corner targets and power play passes
  • Goal horn, red lamp and crowd celebrations
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
