About the game

Tap under the ball to kick it back into the air and do not let it drop. Hit it with your head, chest, knee and foot to build combos, and steer it through the stars. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with wind, hoops, flying frisbees and even two balls at once. Simple to learn, fun to master.

  • Tap to juggle with trick touches
  • Collect stars and fly through hoops
  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new twists
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
