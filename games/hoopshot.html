About the game

Swipe up from the ball to shoot and watch it arc toward the rim. Make the baskets each level asks for before you run out of balls. Two clean swishes in a row light up the fire ball for big cheers. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with moving hoops, beach wind, soft foam pads and gold target hoops.

  • Real ball physics with rim and backboard bounces
  • 100 levels in 5 worlds from the arena to a moonlit beach
  • Swish streaks, fire ball and slow motion baskets
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
