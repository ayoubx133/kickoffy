About the game

Your runner sprints by itself, you just tap JUMP at the right moment to clear each hurdle. Hold for a big leap over the steeplechase water jump, slide under bars in the park, double jump tall stacks and beat moving light bars at night. Perfect jumps give you extra speed to pass your rivals. Win 100 levels in 5 worlds and collect medals and team kits.

  • One tap hurdle jumping with perfect jump speed boosts
  • Water jumps, slides, double jumps and moving light bars
  • Rival runners, photo finishes and podium ceremonies
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
