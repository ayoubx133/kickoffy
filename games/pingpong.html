About the game

Table tennis made simple and fun. Move your paddle under the ball and swipe up through it to hit back. Swipe faster for power and sideways to curve the ball. Play rally drills, hit targets on the far side and win real matches against rivals. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds: the Sports Hall, the Sunny Garden, the Future Arena, the Sunset Garden and the Night Stadium, with target practice, curve balls and high balls you can smash.

  • 100 levels in 5 worlds: drills, targets and matches
  • Swipe to hit with power, curve and smashes
  • Unlock 6 cool paddles with your coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
