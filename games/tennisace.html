About the game

Swipe up to hit the ball over the net and swipe left or right to aim. Hit when the timing ring locks on for a perfect shot that flies faster. Win points, serve aces, land the ball on gold targets and beat two rivals at once in doubles. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a blue hard court to night finals under the lights.

  • Earn coins and stars as you play

  • Plays fully offline
