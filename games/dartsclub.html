About the game

Hold your finger on the screen to steady the wobbling aim, then let go to throw the dart. Hit target scores, rings and numbers in order to clear each challenge. Count down to zero before your rival in friendly duels. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with around the clock, moving boards, duels and balloon boards.

  • Hold and release aiming with a steady meter
  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with duels and balloon boards
  • 6 dart designs to unlock with your coins
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
