Bump, set and smash your way to beach volleyball glory in 2v2 matches!

About the game

Drag on your side of the sand to move under the ball and your team bumps and sets it for you. Tap the rival side to jump and smash the ball right where you tapped. Win rallies to reach the target score before the rival team. Play 100 matches in 5 worlds with sea wind, blockers at the net, gold zones and a fast storm ball.

  • Simple drag and tap volleyball controls
  • 100 matches in 5 worlds on sunny and night beaches
  • Smart rival teams that dig, set and block
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
