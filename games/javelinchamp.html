About the game

Tap RUN to build speed, hold THROW before the foul line and let go at the best angle to send your javelin flying. Stop the power and angle meters in the shot put, time your spins in the discus, hit target rings in the wind and spin the hammer before letting go in the gold zone. Beat the best throws in 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • Javelin, shot put, discus, target throws and hammer
  • Angle, power and spin timing with wind
  • Podium ceremonies, records and team kits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
