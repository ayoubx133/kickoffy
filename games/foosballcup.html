Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Slide your rods and flick to score in table football matches against the CPU.

About the game

Drag left and right to slide your team along the rods, then tap or flick up to kick. Aim with where the ball meets the boot, block the CPU shots and score first to win the match. Bank shots off bumpers, time shots past a sliding goal blocker, handle two balls at once and warp the ball through portals. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds of table themes.

  • Simple slide and flick controls
  • Bumpers, blockers, multi ball and portals
  • Five table themes with their own music
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
