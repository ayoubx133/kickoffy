About the game

Hold anywhere to draw the bow, slide your finger to steady the swaying sight and let go to shoot. Aim a little high for far targets, lean into the wind, lead moving targets, pop floating balloons and time your shot past sliding shields. Score enough points with your arrows to win medals and coins. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a sunny meadow to a lantern lit night range.

  • 100 archery ranges in 5 worlds with new twists
  • Real arrow drop, wind, moving targets and balloons
  • Unlock archers and colorful bows in the locker room
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
