About the game

Tap on the mark to jump, hold to tuck and spin, then let go to open up and land on your feet. Balance on the beam, let go of the bars in the green zone, tap the board and the vault table and swipe up to add twists. Perform routines across 100 levels in 5 worlds and win the judges over with perfect landings. Six bright leotard skins, sparkling ribbons and a graceful waltz make every routine shine.

  • Floor, beam, bars and vault events
  • Hold to flip, let go to stick the landing
  • Judges score every element
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
