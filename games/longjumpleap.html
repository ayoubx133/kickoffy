About the game

Tap RUN fast to build speed, then press JUMP at the take off board. Hold to choose your angle and let go to fly into the sand pit. Hop, step and jump in the triple jump, land inside glowing target zones in the wind, clear the high jump bar with a perfect arch and vault over the bar with a bending pole. Beat the gold mark in 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • Long jump, triple jump, high jump and pole vault
  • Speed, timing and angle control with wind and target zones
  • Gold medals, personal records and new team kits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
