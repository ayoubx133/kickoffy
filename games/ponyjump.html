About the game

Ride a happy pony around a show jumping course. Tap to jump the fences, hold for a big jump over water and wide oxers, tap the duck pad under low banners and hop over rolling hay bales. Ride clear rounds through 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a sunny show arena to a country cross course, a snowy arena, a sunset trail and a moonlight derby. Unlock six pony colours and cheer with the crowd on every clean jump.

  • Simple tap and hold jumping
  • Water, oxers, banners and rolling hay bales
  • Six pony skins and clear round medals
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
