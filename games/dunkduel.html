About the game

Hold to jump and let go at the top of your jump to shoot or slam dunk. Jump at the right moment to block shots and tap close to a rival to steal the ball. Be the first to reach the target score in every match. Play 100 matches in 5 worlds with power bubbles, moving hoops, shot clocks, giant rivals and bounce pads.

  • One button jump, shoot, block and dunk controls
  • 100 matches in 5 worlds against smarter rivals
  • 6 players to unlock with the coins you win
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
