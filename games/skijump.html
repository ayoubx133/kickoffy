About the game

Tap to push off from the gate and race down the icy in-run. Tap at the edge to jump, hold to lean forward and glide, then let go just before the snow to land a clean telemark. Fly past the gold line, through gold rings, into the landing zone and around balloons and birds. 100 levels in 5 worlds, from the alpine hill to an aurora night.

  • One finger flight: tap, hold, let go
  • Rings, landing zones, wind gusts and floating balloons
  • Cheer with a trophy ceremony after every great jump
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
