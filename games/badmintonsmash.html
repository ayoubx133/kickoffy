About the game

Tap the spot on the other side of the net where you want the shuttle to land, right when the ring locks on. Tap near the net for a soft drop or far back for a high clear, and smash the gold shuttles. Play in the wind, send shots through floating hoops and beat a lightning fast rival. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a sports hall to night lights.

  • Tap to aim: drops, clears and smashes
  • Wind, hoops and target challenges
  • Floaty shuttle physics with satisfying smashes
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
