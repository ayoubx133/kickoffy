About the game

You are the quarterback. Drag on the field to aim ahead of your receiver and let go to throw a perfect spiral. Lead your receivers past defenders who read your throw, hit swinging tire targets, beat the blitz and throw on the run. Complete enough passes in each match to win. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from game day to the snow night final.

  • Lead your receivers with a clear aim guide
  • Tire targets, blitzes and roll out throws
  • Smart defenders that break on the ball
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
