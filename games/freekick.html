About the game

Draw your shot from the ball to the goal and the ball follows your curve. Bend it around the defender wall, over it or low under a jumping wall and past the keeper. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds with glowing targets, moving walls, wind and night matches. Every free kick is from a new spot, so every level is a fresh puzzle.

  • Draw the curve and watch the ball bend
  • Beat walls, keepers, targets and wind
  • 100 levels in 5 worlds with new twists
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
