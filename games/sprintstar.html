About the game

Tap the LEFT and RIGHT pads in turn to make your runner sprint down the track. Wait for the green light for a perfect start and tap LEAN at the line to win close photo finishes. Pass the baton in relays, pace yourself in long races, turn fast in shuttle runs and tap on the beat to fill your turbo. Win gold medals in 100 levels in 5 worlds and unlock new team kits.
