About the game

Swipe in the direction you want the ball to go. A long swipe lofts it high for a six, a short swipe keeps it on the ground for a four. Time your shot as the ball arrives, watch out for spin, bouncers and yorkers, and find the gaps between the fielders. Play 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a stadium day game to pink ball nights.

  • Swipe to choose the direction and the height of your shot
  • Pace, spin, bouncers and yorkers to read
  • Fielders, six zones and big crowd cheers
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
