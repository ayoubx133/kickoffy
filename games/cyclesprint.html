About the game

Hold the PEDAL pad to ride fast and let go to rest before your legs burn. Slide your finger up or down to change lanes, ride behind rivals to save energy and fill your SPRINT meter, then sprint for the finish. Climb mountain hills, dodge cones, grab water bottles and survive elimination laps at night. Win 100 levels in 5 worlds.

  • Pedal and rest energy control with sprint finishes
  • Drafting, lane steering, hills, cones and water bottles
  • Elimination races, podium ceremonies and team kits
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
