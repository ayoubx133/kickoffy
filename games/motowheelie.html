About the game

Hold to open the throttle and lift the front wheel, let go to bring it back down. Keep the needle in the green and hold the wheelie through every zone without touching down or flipping over. Ride 100 levels in 5 worlds: a desert highway, city streets with bumps and holes, rolling country hills, sunset roads full of stars to grab and a night run with speed gates. Earn coins and unlock new bikes and riders.

  • One finger throttle with real balance physics
  • Bumps, hills, star lines and speed gates
  • Balance meter, speedometer and medal ceremonies
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
