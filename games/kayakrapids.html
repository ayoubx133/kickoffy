About the game

Hold to paddle and steer toward your finger. Ride the river through slalom gates, dodge rocks, use the fast chutes, stay away from whirlpools and drop down the green chute of every waterfall. Finish before the clock runs out or race a rival kayak. 100 levels in 5 worlds, from a forest river to a lantern night.

  • Hold and drag controls with real river currents
  • Gates, chutes, whirlpools, drops and a rival kayak
  • Beat the time for 3 stars on every river
  • Earn coins and stars as you play
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new models
  • Plays fully offline
